01 — The problem
You fund before you transact
To pay out naira in Nigeria, you need naira in a Nigerian bank account before the transaction happens. That float is locked up, earning nothing, and losing value every day the naira moves.
02 — The HyperFX approach
Match liquidity on demand
Instead of pre-funding, HyperFX matches your USDT or USDC to cNGN liquidity at the moment of transaction. USD → USDT → cNGN → naira bank account.
03 — The result
Capital back in your business
Your working capital stays on your balance sheet. Your settlements remain CBN-compliant. Your FX ops team stops manually managing LP relationships. You can now scale without the float overhead.
